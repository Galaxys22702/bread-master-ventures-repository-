# Housing Assessment Framework

This is a planning framework, not a clinical or legal assessment.

## Intake areas
1. Current housing situation
2. Immediate safety or shelter needs
3. Household/dependants
4. Income and employment situation
5. Identification/documents available
6. Transportation needs
7. Existing benefits or services
8. Barriers to stable housing
9. Preferred housing area/type
10. Referrals and follow-up actions

## Privacy
Collect only information needed to provide the service. Define who can access records, how long they are kept, and how consent is obtained before collecting sensitive information.

## Outcome
The assessment should produce:
- priority needs
- referrals
- responsible staff/volunteer
- follow-up date
- status
