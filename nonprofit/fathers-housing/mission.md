# Fathers Housing & Community Programme

## Draft mission
Support fathers working towards stable housing, employment, family stability and self-sufficiency by connecting them with practical services, referrals and community-based support.

## Potential service areas
- housing assessment and referrals
- transitional/supportive housing planning
- employment and skills support
- transportation assistance
- food and clothing assistance
- parenting/family support
- identification and benefits referrals
- community resource navigation

## Important
The final charitable purpose and programme design must be reviewed before formal nonprofit filings or fundraising.
