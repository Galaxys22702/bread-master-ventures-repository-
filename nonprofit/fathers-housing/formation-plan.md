# Nonprofit Formation Plan

## Before filing
- define charitable purpose
- choose final name
- identify initial directors
- define governance and conflict-of-interest rules
- create an initial programme budget
- confirm whether the organisation will operate housing directly or provide navigation/referrals
- document separation from Bread + Master for-profit ventures

## Formation work
Typical work includes:
- state entity formation
- EIN
- bylaws
- board resolutions
- conflict-of-interest policy
- federal tax-exemption application if appropriate
- state/local registrations that apply to fundraising or operations
- bank account and bookkeeping
- insurance
- programme/privacy policies

## Financial separation
The nonprofit must have its own bank account, books, contracts and decision records. Payments involving related for-profit businesses require documented, fair and properly approved arrangements.

## Status
Planning only. Verify current legal and filing requirements before submission.
