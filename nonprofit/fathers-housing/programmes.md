# Programme Outline

## Housing Stability
Assessment, referral, housing-navigation support and follow-up.

## Employment
Job-search support, training referrals, resume help and employer connections.

## Transportation
Bus passes, transport referrals and future vehicle-related assistance if sustainable.

## Essential Needs
Food, clothing, hygiene items and child-related essentials through donations or partner organisations.

## Fatherhood & Family Stability
Parenting resources, peer support and referrals to appropriate family/community services.

## Measurement
Track practical outcomes such as:
- housing placements
- employment placements
- referrals completed
- programme participation
- repeat housing crises
