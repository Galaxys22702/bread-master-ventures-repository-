# Car Wash Business Plan

## Concept
To be defined: self-service, automatic, hand wash/mobile, or hybrid.

## Customer
Define the target customer and service area.

## Revenue
Potential sources:
- individual washes
- monthly memberships
- detailing/upgrades
- fleet accounts
- vending/air/vacuum services

## Key questions
- What model has the best fit for the target location?
- What is the expected daily vehicle count?
- What are water, sewer and power costs?
- What environmental or discharge requirements apply?
- Buy, lease or build?
- What labour is required?

## Status
Research stage.
