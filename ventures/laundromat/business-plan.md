# Laundromat Business Plan

## Concept
Neighbourhood self-service laundromat with optional add-on services.

## Potential revenue
- washers
- dryers
- wash-and-fold
- vending
- detergent sales
- pickup/delivery, if later justified

## Key questions
- How many households/renters are in the service area?
- How close are existing laundromats?
- What are water, sewer, gas and electrical costs?
- What machine mix is appropriate?
- What lease length is required to justify equipment investment?
- Attended or unattended?

## Status
Research stage.
