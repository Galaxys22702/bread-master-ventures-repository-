# Vending Machine Business Plan

## Concept
Start with a small number of machines and prove route economics before scaling.

## Possible locations
- workplaces
- apartment communities
- laundromats
- auto shops
- community facilities
- warehouses
- gyms or other high-traffic sites where permitted

## Revenue model
Revenue per machine minus:
- product cost
- location commission/rent
- card-processing fees
- fuel/route cost
- spoilage/theft
- repairs

## Key questions
- Which product mix fits each site?
- Is there a written placement agreement?
- What minimum sales justify keeping a machine?
- How often must it be serviced?
- Cashless, cash, or both?

## Status
Research stage.
