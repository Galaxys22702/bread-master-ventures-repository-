# Roles and Decisions

## Current management
Permanent managers have not been assigned.

**Bread and Master manage the ventures jointly while they are being developed.**

## Temporary task ownership
Every active task should have one named owner, even when both partners are involved.

Example:
- Equipment quotes — Bread
- Location research — Master
- Final decision — joint approval

## Decisions requiring joint approval
- Taking on debt
- Accepting an investor
- Signing a lease
- Buying major equipment
- Opening or closing a venture
- Changing ownership percentages
- Moving money between entities
- Hiring a permanent manager

## Recordkeeping
Important decisions should be recorded in writing with:
- date
- decision
- who approved it
- estimated cost
- next action
