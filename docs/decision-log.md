# Decision Log

Use this file for material decisions affecting Bread + Master ventures or the separate nonprofit initiative.

Do not put confidential account information, personal IDs or sensitive participant data here.

## What belongs here

Record decisions such as:

- selecting or dropping a venture
- changing the order of priorities
- approving major equipment purchases
- signing or rejecting a lease
- taking on debt
- accepting an investor
- changing ownership arrangements
- hiring a permanent manager
- changing the nonprofit programme model
- approving a related-party transaction

Small research notes and routine task updates belong in the relevant venture file instead.
