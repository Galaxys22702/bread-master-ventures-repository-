# Master Plan

## Purpose
Build a group of practical, cash-generating small businesses while separately developing a fathers-focused nonprofit/community housing programme.

## Current business priorities
1. Vending machines
2. Laundromat
3. Car wash

## Why this order
Vending can usually be tested with less capital and fewer fixed costs. Laundromats and car washes generally require heavier equipment, utilities, property commitments and permitting.

## Shared rules
- Bread and Master manage jointly for now.
- Assign one temporary owner to each active task.
- Keep written records of decisions and spending.
- Validate demand before major purchases.
- Keep business and nonprofit funds completely separate.
- Do not accept investor money until ownership, terms and legal structure are documented.

## Immediate planning goals
- Estimate startup capital for each venture.
- Identify viable locations.
- Compare equipment purchase, lease and used-equipment options.
- Build simple revenue and break-even models.
- Document licensing, insurance and utility requirements.
