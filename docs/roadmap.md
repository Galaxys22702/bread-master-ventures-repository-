# Bread + Master Roadmap

This roadmap tracks planning work. It is not a promise to launch every concept.

## Stage 1 — Organise

- [x] Create a central repository.
- [x] Separate for-profit and nonprofit planning.
- [x] Create initial business-plan, location and startup-cost files.
- [x] Document joint-management rules.
- [x] Add repository security and contribution guidance.

## Stage 2 — Validate

### Vending machines

- [ ] Define the first machine and product model.
- [ ] Build a location lead list.
- [ ] Record placement terms and commissions.
- [ ] Gather machine, reader, inventory and transport quotes.
- [ ] Build per-machine revenue and break-even assumptions.
- [ ] Decide the minimum performance required to keep a location.

### Laundromat

- [ ] Identify candidate service areas.
- [ ] Record competing laundromats and nearby renter density.
- [ ] Verify water, sewer, gas and electrical requirements.
- [ ] Compare used, new, leased and financed equipment.
- [ ] Build a preliminary machine mix and break-even model.
- [ ] Define acceptable lease requirements before negotiating.

### Car wash

- [ ] Choose the model to research first: self-service, automatic, hand wash/mobile or hybrid.
- [ ] Define minimum site and access requirements.
- [ ] Verify water, sewer, drainage and power needs.
- [ ] Research environmental, discharge, zoning and permit requirements.
- [ ] Gather equipment and site-development estimates.
- [ ] Build a preliminary vehicle-count and break-even model.

### Fathers housing/community initiative

- [ ] Choose a final working name.
- [ ] Finalise the initial charitable/programme scope.
- [ ] Decide whether the programme will operate housing directly, provide navigation/referrals, or use a phased model.
- [ ] Identify initial directors or governing participants.
- [ ] Draft an initial programme budget.
- [ ] Document conflict-of-interest and related-party rules.
- [ ] Verify current formation, tax-exemption, fundraising and operating requirements before filing.

## Stage 3 — Commit

A venture moves into this stage only after the core assumptions have been checked.

Before signing leases, taking debt, accepting investor money or buying major equipment:

- [ ] Record the decision and joint approval.
- [ ] Confirm startup and working-capital needs.
- [ ] Confirm licences, permits and insurance.
- [ ] Confirm entity and banking structure.
- [ ] Review contracts before signing.
- [ ] Identify the temporary operating owner for launch tasks.

## Stage 4 — Launch and measure

After launch, replace assumptions with actual numbers.

Track:

- revenue
- operating costs
- cash flow
- customer/location performance
- repairs and downtime
- labour or service time
- outstanding risks
- next expansion decision

Keep confidential financial records outside this public repository.
